# Product Vision

## One-sentence definition

A browser-based, online idle strategy RPG where players manage characters, parties, equipment and progression across a large interactive world rather than manually controlling every combat action.

## An online idle RPG

Global Idle is an **online** game.

- combat is automated and simulated by the server, never by the browser;
- Hunts and Dungeons progress only while the player's session is connected - a background or
  minimized tab still counts as connected;
- an unexpected disconnect pauses the activity for 5 minutes before ending it, and nothing
  progresses while it is paused;
- offline progression is limited to dedicated Skill Training.

"Idle" describes how combat is played, not the absence of a session.

## What makes the game different

The project combines:

- idle combat;
- RPG progression;
- navigable world map;
- party strategy;
- randomized equipment;
- item sinks;
- dungeon-style quests;
- repeatable boss progression;
- player economy.

## Player fantasy

The player should feel like they are managing an adventuring roster inside a persistent RPG world.

The interesting decisions are:

- where to hunt;
- which monsters are efficient;
- which element/build to use;
- which character belongs in which party;
- what supplies to bring;
- whether to risk a harder area;
- whether to sell or keep an item;
- whether to Forge it;
- whether to use it as sacrifice;
- when to unlock quests/services;
- how to allocate progression resources.

## Design pillars

### 1. Strategy over manual execution
Combat is automated, but preparation and progression decisions remain meaningful.

### 2. Equipment is exciting
Any equipment drop can matter because rarity and affixes are instance-based.

### 3. World identity matters
Hunts are attached to places on a world map.

### 4. Progression has consequences
Death, supplies, unlocks, Forge costs and market fees create friction.

### 5. Classes have identities
Not every class should become equally good at everything.

### 6. Economy matters
Items leave the economy through Forge and other sinks; currencies leave through progression systems and fees.

### 7. Free must remain viable
Premium improves convenience/capacity, not the player's right to compete.

## Non-goals

- not a traditional WASD/MMO client;
- not a manual tile-by-tile recreation of a full MMORPG;
- not a pure clicker;
- not a stage-only menu with no world context;
- not client-authoritative;
- not pay-to-win by exclusive combat power.
